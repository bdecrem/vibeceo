Thread is a low-power wireless mesh networking protocol designed for IoT/smart home devices (like sensors, lights, locks) that lets them talk to each other reliably without needing WiFi or Bluetooth, and it's the underlying network layer for Matter (the new universal smart home standard).

Matter is a universal smart home standard backed by Apple, Google, Amazon, and Samsung that lets smart devices        
  (lights, locks, thermostats, etc.) work together regardless of brand or ecosystem — so a device that works with Alexa
  also works with HomeKit and Google Home, out of the box. Thread (which you asked about earlier) is one of the         
  networking layers it runs on.

ou're thinking of RTSP (Real Time Streaming Protocol) — and yes, I can confirm it does! I already pulled the full specs from the Tapo website earlier. The C560WS lists both:

RTSP — yes ✅
ONVIF — yes ✅
RTSP lets you stream the camera feed directly into third-party software like VLC, Home Assistant, Blue Iris, or an NVR system. ONVIF is the industry standard protocol that makes it compatible with most professional security systems. Both are the same as the original TC42 you were looking at.
